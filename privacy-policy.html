Privacy Policy — Learn Day Trading

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Learn Day Trading is an educational app that teaches the basics of day trading through an 8-phase course, practice simulators, and risk-management tools. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Learn Day Trading does not create user accounts, does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. The app does not save your progress or quiz results anywhere — nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

How the app works

The app runs entirely on your device and works offline. No personal data is transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself.

Third-party services

The app loads its display fonts from Google Fonts when it starts. Like any web request, this means Google's servers receive a standard request (including your IP address) at that moment. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The app also contains links to external educational references (for example, FINRA.org articles). Tapping one opens that site in your browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is rated for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
